# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Platform Admin**:
A member of the Cadence team who runs the platform and decides Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own), Staff

**Team Admin**:
A team member who manages that team's Seats. A Team Admin can raise a Refund Request on their team's Team Licence, but never decides one.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own)

**Buyer**:
The person who made a Purchase: a learner buying for themselves, or a Team Admin buying a Team Licence. Only the Buyer can raise a Refund Request on it.
_Avoid_: Customer, Purchaser

### Purchases

**Purchase**:
One payment by one Buyer for one course, either for themselves or as a Team Licence.
_Avoid_: Order, Enrollment

**Team Licence**:
A Purchase of several Seats in one course, bought by a Team Admin for their team.
_Avoid_: Bulk purchase, Team purchase

**Seat**:
One place on a Team Licence that a team member can claim to get access to the course.
_Avoid_: Coupon, Code (for the place itself)

**Claimed Seat**:
A Seat a team member has taken. A Team Licence with any Claimed Seat can never be refunded, and a Refund Request on it can never be approved.

**Refunded Purchase**:
A Purchase with an Approved Refund Request. It no longer gives access to the course, its unclaimed Seats can no longer be claimed, but the learner's progress, comments and ratings are kept, and they may make a new Purchase of the same course.
_Avoid_: Cancelled, Ended, Revoked

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Buyer may raise a Refund Request on a Purchase right now. A Purchase for oneself has it while it is within fourteen days, under two hours watched, under half the lessons completed, and not already a Refunded Purchase. A Team Licence has it while it is within fourteen days, has no Claimed Seat, and is not already a Refunded Purchase. Once a request is raised, these rules inform the Platform Admin's decision but don't decide it.
_Avoid_: Refundable, Refund window (for the whole set of rules)

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Buyer's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a Purchase. A Platform Admin approves or declines it, and the decision is final. One Purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Platform Admin has decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Platform Admin has accepted. Approving it creates a Pending Refund and makes its Purchase a Refunded Purchase.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Platform Admin has turned down, always with a note explaining why. It has no Refund, and the Buyer may raise a new Refund Request only while the Purchase still has Refund Eligibility.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a Buyer after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund the team has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet. Every Refund starts here, the moment its Refund Request is approved.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the Buyer.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.
