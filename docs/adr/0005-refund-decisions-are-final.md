# Refund decisions are final

A Platform Admin's decision on a Refund Request can't be edited or reversed in the app. Declining requires a note explaining why; a note on an approval is optional. If two admins decide the same request at once, the first decision wins and the second admin is told it's already decided. A Buyer who thinks a decline was wrong can raise a new Refund Request, but only while the Purchase still has Refund Eligibility. After that, it's a matter for customer support outside the app.

## Considered Options

- **Let admins reverse decisions.** Rejected: reversing an approval would mean granting access again and stopping a Refund that may already be moving.
- **Measure the window for a new request from the first request.** Rejected for now: it gives a wrongly declined Buyer a way back, but it makes the window harder to explain. Revisit if support ends up dealing with a lot of these.
