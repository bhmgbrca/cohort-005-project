# A refunded learner may buy again, and their old progress counts

A Refunded Purchase is finished: no new Refund Request can be raised on it. The learner may make a new Purchase of the same course, which starts a new 14-day window. Their kept progress counts toward the new Purchase's watched-hours and half-the-lessons limits. Access checks that need a Purchase, such as the PPP country check, use the latest Purchase.

## Considered Options

- **Reset the limits on each Purchase.** Rejected: someone could buy, watch, get a refund and repeat as often as they like.
- **Ban buying again after a refund.** Rejected: it punishes learners who weren't ready the first time.
